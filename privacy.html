Privacy policy: ACC Status Engine

Last updated 20 November 2026.

Who uses it

ACC Status Engine is a private internal tool of Applied Capability Consulting, Malaysia. Its only user is the owner of the business, signed in with his own Google account.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the tool reads and writes the owner's own Google Sheets, Google Docs and Google Drive files, and creates email drafts in the owner's own Gmail, to manage his business records (engagements, claims, invoices and payments).

What it does with that data

The data stays in the owner's Google account. One exception: when the owner asks the tool to tidy a client note, the text of that note is sent to OpenAI's API to do the tidying, and the result is saved back to the owner's Google Doc. Nothing else is sent outside Google. The data is not sold or used for advertising. No data is collected from anyone other than the owner.
